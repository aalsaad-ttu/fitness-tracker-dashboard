About This Tracker

Many fitness apps include too many features or require paid subscriptions. This tracker focuses only on the essentials so users can quickly log meals and understand how much of their daily calorie target they have used.

This version improves on Project 01 by giving users more control over their entries. Meals can now be added and removed, making the tracker more useful and realistic for everyday use.
